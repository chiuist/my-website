# ThenDo 1.3

- 官网版新增应用内更新：右键菜单「检查更新…」可查看当前版本并手动检查，启动时和每 6 小时也会自动检查。
- 发现新版本后可在应用内下载、安装并重启；也可以稍后再说或跳过该版本。
- 商店版发现新版本时提示前往 App Store 更新。
- 删除任务前先确认，取消则保留任务。
- 隐私政策补充检查更新所涉及的网络请求说明。

旧版官网安装包没有内置更新功能，需要手动安装一次本版本；之后就能在应用内更新。

# ThenDo 1.3

- The website edition can now update itself: choose Check for Updates… from the menu to see your current version and check manually; ThenDo also checks at launch and every 6 hours.
- When a new version is available, download, install, and relaunch from inside the app, or choose Later or Skip This Version.
- The App Store edition points you to the App Store when a new version is available.
- Deleting a task now asks for confirmation; cancelling keeps the task.
- The privacy policy now explains the network requests used to check for updates.

Older website builds have no built-in updater, so install this version manually once; after that, updates happen in the app.
