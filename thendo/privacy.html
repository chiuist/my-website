隐私政策

ThenDo · 生效日期及最后更新：2026 年 9 月 10 日

ThenDo 由 Hong 开发，是一款在 Mac 本机运行的菜单栏待办应用。本政策说明应用内数据、支持邮件及相关网页的处理方式。

应用不收集数据，不追踪用户。
待办标题、日期和完成状态保存在你的 Mac 上，不会由 ThenDo 上传到开发者或第三方服务器。

1. 保存在本机的数据

ThenDo 为提供单项及顺序待办功能，在应用沙盒中保存待办标题、所选日期、完成状态、创建时间、条目标识及分组顺序。数据用于展示、编辑、按顺序完成待办，以及展示已完成事项。ThenDo 不提供账号或云同步，也不读取通讯录、系统日历、照片、位置或其他应用中的数据。

2. 收集、使用与分享

ThenDo 不通过应用收集或上传待办内容、身份信息、设备标识或使用行为，不包含广告、分析或第三方追踪 SDK。我们没有接收待办数据的服务器，也不会出售或分享这些本机数据。待办功能无需网络连接即可使用。检查更新时，官网版连接 then-do.chiuist.com 获取版本信息和更新包，商店版连接 Apple 查询可用版本。这些请求不包含待办内容；服务提供方可能处理 IP 地址、应用版本及提供更新所需的请求信息。

3. 保存期限与删除

待办数据保存在本机，直到你删除相关事项或清除本机数据。已完成事项会继续保留在已完成列表；卸载应用不一定会移除沙盒数据。若要清除包括已完成事项在内的所有本机记录，请先退出 ThenDo，再在访达的「前往文件夹」中打开以下位置并删除 todos.json：

~/Library/Containers/com.heyhong.thendo/Data/Library/Application Support/ThenDo/

清除后应用将从空列表开始，删除无法通过 ThenDo 撤销。若你使用了 Time Machine 或其他系统备份，备份副本由你使用的备份工具管理。我们没有远程副本，无法替你恢复或远程删除本机事项。

4. 主动联系支持时的信息

如果你通过电子邮件联系我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及主动提供的消息、附件或问题描述，仅用于回复和处理支持请求。请勿发送与问题无关的个人或敏感内容。相关通信由邮件服务提供商处理，我们按解决问题所需的期限保留支持记录，法律要求保留的情况除外。你可以使用下方邮箱请求删除你发送给我们的支持信息。

5. 网页与 Apple 系统服务

本隐私政策 HTML 不包含 Cookie、统计脚本、外部字体或追踪资源。当你通过浏览器访问线上页面时，网站托管或网络服务可能处理提供网页所需的 IP 地址及请求信息；这取决于网站服务的配置，不代表 ThenDo 应用上传了待办数据。App Store、系统诊断或备份服务由 Apple 或相应服务提供者按照其政策管理。

6. 儿童隐私

ThenDo 面向一般用户，不以儿童为特定目标群体。应用不向我们传输个人数据。若你认为通过支持邮件向我们提供了不应保留的儿童个人信息，请联系我们删除。

7. 政策更新与联系

若数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及上方日期，并在适用要求下提供必要通知或取得同意。

Privacy Policy

ThenDo · Effective and last updated: September 10, 2026

ThenDo is a Mac menu bar todo app developed by Hong. This policy explains how local app data, support emails, and related web pages are handled.

The app does not collect data or track you.
Your todo titles, dates, and completion states stay on your Mac. ThenDo does not upload them to the developer or third-party servers.

1. Data stored on your Mac

To provide single and sequential todos, ThenDo stores titles, selected dates, completion states, creation times, item identifiers, and group order in its app sandbox. This data is used to display, edit, complete, and show completed todos. ThenDo has no account or cloud sync and does not read contacts, system calendars, photos, location, or data from other apps.

2. Collection, use, and sharing

ThenDo does not collect or upload todo content, identity information, device identifiers, or usage activity through the app. It includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We have no server that receives your todos, and we do not sell or share this local data. Todo features work without a network connection. Update checks contact then-do.chiuist.com for website releases or Apple for App Store releases. These requests contain no todo content; providers may process IP addresses, app versions, and request information needed to deliver updates.

3. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your Mac until you delete the relevant items or remove the local data. Completed todos remain in the completed list. Uninstalling the app may not remove its sandbox data. To erase all local records, including completed todos, quit ThenDo, use Finder’s Go to Folder command to open the following folder, and delete todos.json:

~/Library/Containers/com.heyhong.thendo/Data/Library/Application Support/ThenDo/

The app will then start with an empty list. ThenDo cannot undo this deletion. Copies in Time Machine or other backups are managed by the backup tools you use. We hold no remote copy and cannot restore or remotely delete your local todos.

4. Information you send to support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any messages, attachments, or details you choose to provide. We use them only to respond to and resolve your request. Please avoid sending unrelated personal or sensitive information. Email providers process these communications. We retain support records only as needed to handle the request, unless retention is required by law. You can ask us to delete information you have sent by contacting the email address below.

5. Web pages and Apple services

This privacy policy HTML includes no cookies, analytics scripts, external fonts, or tracking resources. When you visit the hosted page in a browser, hosting or network providers may process your IP address and request information as needed to serve the page, depending on their configuration. This does not mean that ThenDo uploads your todos. The App Store, system diagnostics, and backup services are managed by Apple or their respective providers under their own policies.

6. Children’s privacy

ThenDo is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not transmit personal data to us. Please contact us if you believe support correspondence contains a child’s personal information that should be removed.

7. Changes and contact

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, and provide notices or request consent where required.
