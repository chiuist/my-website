# DropEdge 2.2

- 新增「登录时启动」，支持查看系统批准状态。
- 新增托盘操作撤销与重做，误移除、清空后可恢复条目。
- 完善方向键选择、空格预览、Delete 移除等键盘操作与焦点提示。
- 修正自动收起逻辑：成功拖出后移除条目，自动收起只控制托盘是否隐藏。
- 精简设置页，保留底部胶囊质感并缩短图文间距，修正提示框文字的垂直居中。
- 优化文件不可用、数量超限与部分交付的说明，支持重新检查文件。
- 晃动灵敏度改为低、标准、高三个有效档位。

官网版为免费版；Pro、试用和购买恢复请在 Mac App Store 版本中使用。

---

- Added launch at login with system approval status.
- Added Undo and Redo for shelf actions, including removal and clearing.
- Improved keyboard navigation, Space preview, Delete removal, and focus indicators.
- Corrected auto-hide behavior: successful deliveries remove their items; auto-hide controls shelf visibility only.
- Simplified Settings, preserved the action capsules with tighter icon spacing, and centered notification text vertically.
- Improved unavailable-file, capacity, and partial-delivery feedback, with an option to check files again.
- Replaced the shake sensitivity slider with three effective levels: Low, Standard, and High.

The website edition is free. Pro, trials, and purchase restoration are available in the Mac App Store edition.
