# DropEdge 2.1

- 优化「一并拖出」胶囊：保持单行显示，增加细腻高光与阴影，改善文字对比度。
- 调整暗色与透明玻璃外观，减少刺眼的蓝色，提升浅色背景上的可读性。
- 改进文件拖入、批量打包和图片转换的稳定性，完善取消操作与临时文件清理。
- 修复清空后异步文件重新出现、重复拖入误触发数量限制，以及部分文件接收失败时缺少提示的问题。
- 正确显示包含百分号的文件名和带图片后缀的文件夹，优化悬停、键盘与辅助功能体验。

---

- Refined the Drag All capsule with a single-line label, subtle highlights and shadows, and clearer text.
- Improved dark and transparent glass appearances with softer accent colors and better readability over bright backgrounds.
- Improved file importing, ZIP creation, and image conversion, including cancellation and temporary-file cleanup.
- Fixed files reappearing after clearing, duplicate drops incorrectly triggering the item limit, and missing feedback for partially failed imports.
- Corrected filenames containing percent signs and folders with image extensions; refined hover, keyboard, and accessibility behavior.
