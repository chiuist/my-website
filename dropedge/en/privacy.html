DropEdge

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 24, 2026 · Last updated: August 26, 2026

DropEdge is a local-first macOS file-dragging shelf. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data through the App.

1. Scope

This policy explains how DropEdge (the “App”) handles files, images, web links, local settings, mouse-drag events, and in-app purchase information.

2. Local File and Content Processing

Files, folders, images, and web links you drag into DropEdge are processed on your Mac. DropEdge does not upload them to us or to third-party servers. The App reads content locally to display names, types, sizes, and thumbnails and to perform the file transfers you initiate.

ZIP compression and JPEG, PNG, and HEIC image conversion use local system frameworks. A file path is written to the system clipboard only when you select “Copy Path”; DropEdge does not read unrelated clipboard content.

When you drag image data from a browser, photo app, or the clipboard, DropEdge may create a temporary image file in the macOS temporary directory so that it can show a preview and let you drag the image out again. Temporary files are removed when you remove an item, clear the shelf, or next launch the App.

3. File Access Permission

DropEdge uses the macOS App Sandbox. The App accesses content only when you select or drag it. Read and write access to user-selected files is used solely for the parking, dragging, compression, format-conversion, and export actions you explicitly request.

4. Mouse-Drag Events

To reveal the shelf at the screen edge or with a cursor shake, DropEdge locally monitors left-button drag and release events and briefly calculates cursor position and direction. The App only inspects the current drag-type identifier to decide whether to show the shelf. These events are not saved to disk or sent to us or third parties. DropEdge does not record keyboard input or create a history of mouse activity.

You can turn off edge or shake activation in Settings, or quit the App to stop the related monitoring.

5. Local Preferences

DropEdge stores necessary settings on your Mac, including appearance, target screen edge, activation methods, shake sensitivity, and automatic hiding. Trial eligibility and start time are determined by verified Apple StoreKit transaction records, not by the local installation date. This information is not sent to us.

6. In-App Purchases

DropEdge Free downloaded from this website does not offer in-app purchases. DropEdge Pro and its 3-day free trial in the Mac App Store edition are provided through Apple StoreKit. Apple handles product display, payment, refunds, transaction receipts, and Apple ID authentication. DropEdge only obtains product information and verifies Pro or trial access locally through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your card number, complete payment information, or Apple ID password, and we do not operate a developer server that receives transaction data.

Apple’s handling of related information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Data Collection, Tracking, and Third-Party SDKs

DropEdge does not collect identifiable data, analytics, advertising data, or personalized marketing data. It does not track you across apps or websites, contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not sell or rent user data.

8. Data Retention

We do not maintain a remote DropEdge user database. Local temporary files generally remain until you remove an item, clear the shelf, or next launch the App. Local preferences remain until you change or reset them or remove the relevant App data. Apple retains App Store transaction records according to its policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

DropEdge is intended for general macOS users and is not directed specifically to children. Because the App does not transmit personal data to us, we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through DropEdge.

10. Security

DropEdge uses the macOS App Sandbox and seeks to limit file processing to your Mac and to content you actively authorize. No software can guarantee absolute security. Only process files you are authorized to access, and keep macOS and the App up to date.

11. Your Choices and Rights

We do not maintain DropEdge user accounts or a remote user database, so there is generally no remote personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You can clear parked items, disable activation methods, quit the App, manage macOS permissions, remove local App data, or contact us with a privacy question.

12. Policy Updates

If our data-handling practices change materially, we will update this policy, its effective date, and the App Store privacy information. If we add analytics, cloud sync, accounts, or a developer server in the future, we will explain the change and obtain any legally required consent before enabling the feature.
